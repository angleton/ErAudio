ErAudio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what ErAudio stores in your browser and what is sent to Google or Cloudflare when you choose optional features.

Using ErAudio without signing in

ErAudio stores preferences, saved videos and listening history in your browser. Local listening history is kept in browser storage on your device and is not sent to ErAudio's history service unless you choose Google sign-in and sync.

Data stored locally can include YouTube video IDs, titles, chapters or timestamps, playback positions and counts, saved locations, and player settings. You can remove this data by clearing ErAudio's site data in your browser.

Optional Google history sync

If you choose Sign in with Google in the main window, Google provides an identity token so ErAudio can authenticate your account and connect TOP history sync. The history service verifies that token and derives a pseudonymous account identifier from the Google account identifier. A verified email address may be returned to ErAudio to show which account is connected and suggest the same account when you optionally connect YouTube.

ErAudio stores reconnection preferences in browser storage and asks Google to reconnect on later visits or after sign-in expires. In the separate-sign-in flow, each successful Google history sign-in also attempts read-only YouTube authorization without consent screens, using the verified account email as a login hint. First-time consent or browser restrictions may require a manual CONNECT YOUTUBE click; failed YouTube attempts do not prevent history sync. In the combined flow, automatic YouTube reconnection requires a previous connection. Identity tokens, YouTube access tokens, and the account login hint are kept in page memory, not saved in browser storage. Signing out clears both ErAudio account sessions and disables automatic Google reconnection until you sign in again; it does not sign you out of Google on other websites or revoke granted permissions.

When sync is enabled, ErAudio sends the following history data to its Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare D1 database: YouTube video IDs, titles, durations, chapter and timestamp labels, played ranges, play times, playback-session identifiers, and play counts. Records are associated with the pseudonymous account identifier and are not publicly viewable.

Sync is optional. While signed out, history remains local to that browser. Signing out does not delete synced history. Use MANAGE HISTORY while signed in to delete the synced history for that Google account after confirmation; use your browser's site-data controls to remove local history. ErAudio's EXPORT and IMPORT controls let you download or add history data.

For a shared computer, you may approve an ErAudio-only session from a trusted Google-signed-in device instead of signing in to Google on the shared computer. The Worker stores a hash of the shared session credential, a pseudonymous account identifier, its expiration, and revocation state. The session can read and add listening history for up to eight hours, but cannot clear account history. You can revoke shared sessions from a trusted device. A local history copy and unsynced listens may remain on the shared computer after sign-out or expiration; private browsing may remove them when its session closes. A compromised shared computer can access or alter history while its ErAudio session is active.

Deployments using separate sign-in do not request YouTube permission for history sync. The combined-sign-in flow requests Google identity and read-only YouTube permission together, but YouTube permission is optional: granting identity alone can connect TOP. If combined authorization fails, an explicit Google sign-in fallback allows TOP-only access.

Optional YouTube features

You may provide your own YouTube Data API key or Google OAuth client ID in API SETUP. These values are stored in your browser's local storage. The hosted app also supplies a public Google client ID as the default for sign-in and optional YouTube authorization; the combined flow always uses its configured history client ID. The API key is used by your browser to request public video information from Google. If you choose CONNECT YOUTUBE (or YOUTUBE LOGIN on a deployment without history sync), ErAudio requests read-only access to your YouTube account to display playlists and video descriptions; it does not change your YouTube account. In the separate-sign-in flow, the YouTube access token is used only for Google API requests and is not sent to the history service. In the combined flow, the Worker receives the access token from Google when exchanging the authorization code and passes it to the browser only if YouTube permission was granted, without storing it; the Worker does not store a refresh token. YouTube authorization failures leave TOP history sync available.

If a loaded video's description contains no timestamps, ErAudio also requests a sample of public YouTube comments to look for a possible tracklist. Comment-derived timestamps and attribution are held in page memory, scheduled for refresh or discard after 30 minutes, and are not saved to listening history or sent to the history service.

Do not enter a Google client secret. You can clear the saved API key and client ID in API SETUP or remove them by clearing ErAudio's site data. Google and YouTube process requests under their own privacy policies.

Service providers

ErAudio uses Google services for sign-in and optional YouTube features, YouTube for video playback and public video information, and Cloudflare for optional private history sync. Data sent to those services is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as ErAudio changes. For privacy questions, use the support email listed for ErAudio in its Google sign-in configuration.
